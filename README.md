# Squid Farm

We build tools that help people bring the stories they imagine to life.

Talewright is grounded in a simple idea: making an interactive story should not require becoming a
software developer or surrendering creative control to the tools. We want to make room for more
people to create, while giving their ideas space to grow.

Our principles are:

- **Put authors first.** The tools should support the work, not get in its way.
- **Make the first step welcoming.** Creative tools can be approachable without limiting what people
  can make with them.
- **Respect creative ownership.** Stories belong to their authors and should be easy to carry forward
  and share.
- **Build with care.** Favor thoughtful, dependable tools that earn trust over unnecessary complexity.
